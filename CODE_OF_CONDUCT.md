# Code of conduct

Ulpaso welcomes people who contribute code, tests, documentation, translations,
benchmarks, or careful bug reports. Participation should remain constructive and
safe regardless of experience, background, identity, language, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work rather than attacking the person doing it.
- Give reproducible evidence for technical claims and accept corrections.
- Make room for contributors who communicate in a second language.
- Do not publish private recordings, transcripts, credentials, or personal data.
- Stop behavior that another participant reasonably identifies as harassment.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or in private communication connected to the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact
channel instead of opening a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical,
with decisions based on the safety and health of the project community.

This policy applies to all Ulpaso-controlled spaces and to public representation
of the project. Maintainers are also subject to it.
